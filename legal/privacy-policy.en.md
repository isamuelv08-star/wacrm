# Saleslid Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 3, 2026**

> ⚠️ **Note for the Saleslid team:** this document is a draft covering the usual requirements for a WhatsApp CRM SaaS platform that integrates WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Calendar, and AI, and to satisfy Google's OAuth verification process. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Before publishing it, we recommend having an Ecuadorian lawyer review it, in particular regarding Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP).

## 1. Who we are

Saleslid ("Saleslid", "we", "us", "our") is a WhatsApp CRM platform that lets businesses manage conversations, contacts, sales opportunities, calendars, and AI-assisted automatic replies.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at **soporte@saleslid.com**.

This policy applies to:
- The **saleslid.com** website
- The **app.saleslid.com** application (the product)
- Any other subdomain or service operated by Saleslid

## 2. What data we collect

Depending on how you use Saleslid, we may collect:

### 2.1 Account data
- Full name, email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text)
- If you sign in with Google: your Google name, email address, and profile picture
- Your business name and your role within your account (owner, admin, agent, viewer)

### 2.2 Your customers' data (your business's contacts)
When you use Saleslid to serve your own customers over WhatsApp or Instagram, we process on your behalf:
- Your contacts' phone numbers and names
- Conversation content (text messages, voice notes, images, documents)
- Conversation metadata (date, time, delivery status)
- Information you enter about your contacts (tags, notes, custom fields, sales deals)

**Important:** with respect to your own customers' data, we act as a **data processor** (we process the data on your behalf and per your instructions); you, as the business using Saleslid, are the **data controller** for that data in relation to your own customers.

### 2.3 Google Calendar data
If you connect Google Calendar from Settings → Integrations, Saleslid accesses — with your explicit authorization through Google's consent flow — the following:
- Your upcoming calendar events (so the AI agent can avoid double-booking appointments)
- The ability to create and update events on your calendar when an appointment is scheduled, either manually from the Calendar page or automatically by the AI agent

**Use of Google API data:** Saleslid's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:
- We only use your Google Calendar data for the features you explicitly enabled (reading availability and creating/updating events)
- We do not use this data for advertising
- We do not transfer this data to third parties, except as necessary to provide you the service (for example, our infrastructure provider, described below) or where required by law
- No human on our team reads your calendar content except to provide technical support you requested, to comply with legal obligations, or for security reasons

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time from Settings → Integrations, or by revoking access directly from your Google account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Disconnecting stops our access to your calendar and deletes the stored access tokens.

### 2.4 Data processed by artificial intelligence
If you enable AI-powered automatic replies, conversation content (and, where applicable, a summary of your calendar) is sent to the AI provider you configured with your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or OpenRouter) to generate a reply. That provider processes this data under its own privacy policy; we recommend reviewing it before enabling this feature.

### 2.5 Technical data
IP address, browser type, pages visited within the application, and usage logs, for security, fraud prevention, and service improvement purposes.

## 3. How we use your data

- To provide the Saleslid service (shared inbox, sales pipelines, calendar, automations, AI replies)
- To authenticate you and keep your account secure
- To send you operational communications (e.g., platform notifications)
- To provide technical support when you request it
- To improve and maintain the platform
- To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data or your contacts' data to third parties.

## 4. Who we share data with

To provide you the service, we share data with:

- **Supabase** (database and authentication) — hosts your account information and your CRM's operational data
- **Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform, Zernio, and/or Dualhook** — to send and receive WhatsApp and Instagram messages, depending on the connection option you choose
- **Google** — only if you connect Google Calendar or sign in with Google, and only for the features described in this policy
- **Your chosen AI provider** (OpenAI, Anthropic, or OpenRouter) — only if you enable AI-powered automatic replies, using your own API key
- Public authorities, when required by law

None of these third parties may use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the contracted service, except as set out in each provider's own policies (e.g., your AI provider's).

## 5. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:
- Encryption of sensitive credentials at rest (e.g., WhatsApp and Google tokens) using AES-256
- Role-based access control within your account
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between your browser and our servers

No system is 100% secure; if we detect a security incident affecting your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

## 6. How long we keep your data

We retain your data while your account is active. If you cancel your account, we delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it for legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.

## 7. Your rights

Under Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection and other applicable regulations, you may ask us to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal exceptions
- Object to or restrict certain uses of your data
- Withdraw your consent at any time (e.g., by disconnecting Google Calendar)

To exercise these rights, contact us at **soporte@saleslid.com**.

## 8. Children

Saleslid is not directed at minors, and we do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years of age.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the date of the last update at the top of this document; if changes are significant, we will notify you by email or within the application.
